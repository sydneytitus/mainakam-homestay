About

Hills, forest and a family home.

Guests stay in a quiet family property in the hills of Chinnakanal, near Munnar. The land runs to 16 acres of cardamom plantation and forest, and walking paths start right behind the house for hikers and nature lovers.

A live-in caretaker is available 24 hours a day to help with anything you need during your stay.
